How it plays

Downtown becomes the game board

Every clue points to something real: a sign, a plaque, a statue, a wall of old brass post office boxes. You walk, look closely, argue it out and build your theory as a crew.

  1. Meet your crew

    Check in with the host, meet the people on your crew and open your case folio. Your crew joins the field app on one phone with a join code.

  2. Work the case

    Walk downtown stop by stop. Each solve releases new evidence and memos, and hints are there when your crew gets stuck.

  3. File your verdict

    Put the pieces together on paper, trace the final path and file one verdict. Then the case closes and the official answer is revealed.

Questions

St. Pete Detective Club, answered

What is St. Pete Detective Club?

St. Pete Detective Club is a live, hosted detective game by City Play Co. Downtown St. Petersburg, Florida is the game board: crews of three to six follow clues on foot past real landmarks, examine evidence and file one verdict on a fictional case.

Is it a scavenger hunt, an escape room or a walking tour?

It is a hosted detective game with elements of a city scavenger hunt. You do not need escape-room experience or local history knowledge. Every answer is out in the open, on the buildings, plaques and statues downtown.

Can I come alone?

Yes. Solo players are welcome. The host introduces everyone and places you on a small crew, so you won't need to bring a group.

Do I need to be good at puzzles?

No. The case mixes observation, conversation and deduction, and hints are built into the app if your crew gets stuck. Curiosity matters more than speed.

Do we need to download an app?

No. Your crew joins a shared field app in the phone's web browser with a join code. At least one phone per crew is needed, and everyone on the crew sees the same progress.

How far do we walk?

Expect about 2 miles of outdoor walking through Downtown St. Pete at a social pace. Wear comfortable shoes and bring water.
